About

RW Electrical Consulting is led by Reece Winikerei, a Registered Professional Engineer of Queensland (RPEQ) and Victoria (RPEV) with a background leading electrical design and delivery for major infrastructure projects, including international airport baggage handling systems spanning Australia, New Zealand and Canada.

Experience covers the full project lifecycle — design development, procurement, manufacturing and factory acceptance testing, through to site installation and commissioning — with a focus on low-voltage switchgear and Motor Control Centre (MCC) design, safety-related control systems, and coordination across multidisciplinary project teams. RW Electrical Consulting brings that same rigour to independent design and consulting engagements.
