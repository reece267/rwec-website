About

RW Electrical Consulting is an independent electrical engineering consultancy providing practical design and documentation support for industrial projects.

Founded by Reece Winikerei, an electrical engineer with over seven years of experience delivering industrial electrical systems across Australia, New Zealand and internationally. His experience spans electrical design, switchboards and MCCs, equipment selection and procurement, through to installation support, commissioning and project handover.

Having worked across all stages of project delivery, RW Electrical Consulting brings a practical understanding of how electrical designs progress from drawings and calculations through to manufacture, installation and final as-built documentation.

The focus is straightforward: clear, compliant and practical electrical design that supports successful project delivery.
